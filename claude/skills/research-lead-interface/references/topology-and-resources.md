Read this when judging collaboration, recursion, efficiency, model differences, or account use.

## Observed topology

Separate configured limits from actual execution.
Report observed Runtime edges, harness-native children, depth, and concurrency with their observation window.
Group sequential retries of one assignment into one lineage.
An attempted spawn establishes an attempt; a successful invocation establishes execution.
A child report establishes availability; an ancestor's use needs a trace or artifact dependency.

For recursion, require an executed child-to-grandchild edge in addition to the root-to-child edge.
For independent teams, inspect shared seeds, artifacts, prompts, and selection paths.
Multiple sessions alone do not establish independence.
Mark missing native descendants and unreadable branches explicitly.

## Useful work

Describe the retained artifact, reproduced check, resolved question, or decision changed by evidence.
Keep candidate volume separate from accepted results.
Inspect failed and rejected attempts for reusable work before discarding a line.

Report operator interventions separately from agent adaptations.
Check recorded messages and their consumption before attributing a change to feedback.
Temporal coincidence alone does not establish that an agent received an outside finding.

## Resources and comparisons

Use denominators and time windows for active agents, failures, idle capacity, and completed work.
Include unobserved or excluded runs when their absence changes the conclusion.
Separate active compute time, elapsed time, waiting, and observation overhead where measured.
Compare actual resources and useful outcomes; configured budgets do not establish equal compute.

Distinguish configured account labels from verified provider principals.
Distinguish recorded usage estimates from billed subscription cost.
Unknown quota, cost, or principal identity remains unknown.
Keep credentials out of reports.

Before declaring a profile or topology better, disclose task, model, account, order, seed, and resource differences.
A single ordered pair can suggest a follow-up; it cannot isolate all those causes.
State which mechanisms executed and what result would refute the proposed advantage.
