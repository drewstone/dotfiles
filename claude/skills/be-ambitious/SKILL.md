# Be ambitious

## Set the bar

Start with the problem the requester wants solved. Read the strongest related work and inspect its actual method, evidence and limitations. A book, paper, working instrument or result in another field can suggest an approach the local literature misses.

For open research, explain what would change if the question were answered. Teams may develop a new representation, test incompatible explanations or build an instrument before anyone knows how to measure progress. Preserve the result that changed the inquiry and where the team went next.

For a performance claim, name the strongest comparable result and our baseline. State the target, the proposed mechanism and what independent evidence would show a gain. Match resources and record material differences between comparisons.

## Go further

Choose the size of the work from what the outcome requires. An architecture change can be the right first experiment. Stay within the granted authority for resources, production changes and other owners’ systems.

## Finish against the bar

Show the artifact, discovery or evidence the requester can use. State the scope of each result, including unexpected findings and what remains open. Verify a claimed gain against the comparison it names.

## Rewrite a prompt

When the requester asks for a rewritten prompt, give the prompt in their voice with the goal, relevant source material, authority and evidence the result needs.

## Log the run

For a project tracking skill use, record the outcome with `skill-run-log /be-ambitious --target "<question>" --verdict <PASS|FAIL|PARTIAL|BLOCKED|ABANDONED> --next /stop`.
