# Site rationale and historical framing

The [Brand design bible](https://github.com/tangle-network/brand/blob/main/docs/design-bible.md) owns current Tangle vision, visual grammar and shared acceptance. This file preserves the earlier site framing and its rationale. The bible replaces the repeated shared visual and acceptance prescriptions. Unmapped site-shape choices remain historical evidence below.

## Use this evidence

Compare a proposed site structure with the user's current decision and real product proof. Historical product names and site-shape choices below do not establish today's inventory or navigation. Read current product owners before changing either.

## Historical product framing

Tangle is serious infrastructure for agents that improve from evidence.

The point of view:

> Agents only improve when the run is observable, reproducible, scored, and promoted from evidence.

Primary product spine:

- Sandbox: where the agent runs.
- Router: how the model call is controlled.
- Intelligence: how traces become diagnosis, evals, and improvement.

Secondary developer surfaces:

- agent-runtime
- agent-eval
- agent-app
- agent-sdk

Examples and applications:

- browser-agent
- blueprint-agent
- tax-agent
- legal-agent
- gtm-agent

Anti-pattern:

Do not show all three layers at once on the homepage. That is an org chart, not a site.

## Historical site-shape choices

The earlier plan kept Home, Blog and Research. It deferred product, agent, protocol, ecosystem and brand pages until they had proof, with operationally required status, security and legal pages retained.

The proposed homepage showed the Sandbox/Router/Intelligence spine through one real run, recent research/blog content and a docs/workbench action when useful. It rejected product/repository/agent catalogs, equal-card loops, step graphics and traffic-light trace tables. These choices described that homepage, not every Tangle product.

The research direction separated calm, light, dated argument from marketing CTAs, SEO comparisons, invented tracks and repeated Research labels. Its rationale remains useful when reviewing the current editorial collection; Brand owns the shared visual roles.

## Blog classification

Blog can carry:

- technical explainers
- comparisons
- implementation notes
- launch notes
- essays

Blog should not pretend to be research unless it has method, evidence, and a falsifiable claim.
